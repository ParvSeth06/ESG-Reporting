Topic Management Disclosures

1. Topic management disclosures
An organization reporting in accordance with the GRI Standards is required to report how it manages each of its
material topics.
An organization that has determined public policy to be a material topic is required to report how it manages the topic
using Disclosure 3-3 in GRI 3: Material Topics 2021 (see clause 1.1 in this section).
This section is therefore designed to supplement – and not replace – Disclosure 3-3 in GRI 3.
1.1 The reporting organization shall report how it manages public policy using Disclosure
REQUIREMENTS
3-3 in GRI 3: Material Topics 2021.
1.2 The reporting organization should report:
RECOMMENDATIONS
1.2.1 the significant issues that are the focus of its participation in public policy
development and lobbying;
1.2.2 its stance on these issues, and any differences between its lobbying positions
and any stated policies, goals, or other public positions.
