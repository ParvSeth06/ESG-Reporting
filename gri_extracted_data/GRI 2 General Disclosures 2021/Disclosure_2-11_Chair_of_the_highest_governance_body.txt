Disclosure 2-11 Chair of the highest governance body

The organization shall:
REQUIREMENTS
a. report whether the chair of the highest governance body is also a senior executive in
the organization;
b. if the chair is also a senior executive, explain their function within the organization’s
management, the reasons for this arrangement, and how conflicts of interest are
prevented and mitigated.
