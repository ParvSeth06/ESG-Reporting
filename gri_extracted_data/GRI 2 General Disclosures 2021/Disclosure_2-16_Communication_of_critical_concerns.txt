Disclosure 2-16 Communication of critical concerns

The organization shall:
REQUIREMENTS
a. describe whether and how critical concerns are communicated to the highest
governance body;
b. report the total number and the nature of critical concerns that were communicated to
the highest governance body during the reporting period.
Critical concerns include concerns about the organization’s potential and actual negative
GUIDANCE
