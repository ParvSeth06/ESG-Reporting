Disclosure 2-28 Membership associations

The organization shall:
REQUIREMENTS
a. report industry associations, other membership associations, and national or
international advocacy organizations in which it participates in a significant role.
The organization may have a significant role in an association or advocacy organization when it
GUIDANCE
holds a position in the governance body, participates in projects or committees, or provides
substantive funding beyond routine membership dues. The role may also be significant when
the organization views its membership as strategic to influencing the mission or objective of the
association that is critical to the organization’s own activities.
