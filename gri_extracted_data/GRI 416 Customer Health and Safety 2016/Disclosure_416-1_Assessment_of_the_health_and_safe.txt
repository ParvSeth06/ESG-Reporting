Disclosure 416-1 Assessment of the health and safety impacts of

product and service categories
The reporting organization shall report the following information:
REQUIREMENTS
a. Percentage of significant product and service categories for which health and safety
impacts are assessed for improvement.
Guidance for Disclosure 416-1
GUIDANCE
This measure helps to identify the existence and range of systematic efforts to address health
and safety across the life cycle of a product or service. In reporting the information in Disclosure
416-1, the reporting organization can also describe the criteria used for the assessment.
