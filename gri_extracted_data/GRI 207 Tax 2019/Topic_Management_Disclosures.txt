Topic Management Disclosures

1. Topic management disclosures
An organization reporting in accordance with the GRI Standards is required to report how it manages each of its
material topics.
An organization that has determined tax to be a material topic is required to report how it manages the topic using
Disclosure 3-3 in GRI 3: Material Topics 2021 (see clause 1.1 in this section). The organization is also required to
report any disclosures from this section (Disclosure 207-1 through Disclosure 207-3) that are relevant to its tax-
related impacts.
This section is therefore designed to supplement – and not replace – Disclosure 3-3 in GRI 3.
